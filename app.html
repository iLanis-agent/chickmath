Why these numbers

The assumptions, stated honestly
ConstantValueWhy
Coop space4 sq ft / birdThe widely used minimum for standard breeds; less and pecking starts
Run space10 sq ft / birdBirds that can move lay better and fight less
Roost bar10 in / birdChickens sleep shoulder to shoulder, but not stacked
Nesting boxes1 per 4 hensThey share, but one box per hen is a waste
Feed0.25 lb / bird / dayA laying hen eats about a quarter pound of layer feed daily
Peak laying5.5 eggs / hen / weekGood layers in year one; year two drops ~15%, and so on
Brooder95°F, minus 5°F / weekUntil fully feathered, around 6 weeks; 65°F floor

Bantams need less, big heritage breeds more. Production varies by breed, daylight and stress - the math plans, the hens decide.
